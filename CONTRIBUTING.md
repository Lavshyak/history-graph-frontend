# Contributing

This project is **proprietary and source-available**. It is **not** open source.
See [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) for the terms. By default, the only thing the general
public may do is **view** the source code.

## Who may contribute

Only people listed in **Schedule A of the [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)** (currently
`@Falny`) may modify the code, keep a public fork, and submit changes back.
We plan to replace this arrangement with a written contract; once signed, that
contract governs and overrides this document.

## How to submit changes

1. Forking is allowed for listed collaborators only. Keep the fork **public** and
   keep the `LICENSE` and all copyright notices intact.
2. Work on your changes, then open a pull request to the `main` branch.
3. Keep commits focused and describe *why* the change is needed.
